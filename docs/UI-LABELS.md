# Shared robot status labels

Applies to **YAM**, **SO101**, and **Bimanual SO101**. Edit the labels and meanings below.

1. **Ready for the next run:** The robot is in its home position with auto-queue enabled and ready to accept work.
2. **Queued — waiting for your turn:** The robot is in its home position with auto-queue enabled; your prompt is in the queue waiting for assignment.
3. **Queued — waiting for robot readiness:** The robot isn’t ready yet.
4. **Preparing:** Getting ready for your task.
5. **Running:** Your task is active.
6. **Moving home:** Moving to its starting pose.
7. **Parking:** Moving to its resting pose before disabling.
8. **Stopped:** Waiting for operator readiness.
9. **Offline:** Disconnected.
10. **Fault:** A problem needs attention.
11. **Checking / unavailable:** Current status is unknown.
